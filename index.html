FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I pay for ads?

In the beginning, no. During Phase 1 and 2, we focus on engineering your organic authority and building your Trust Ecosystem. Once your foundation is highly converting, we introduce strategic Meta & Google Ads in Phase 3 (The Amplifier) to scale your results. Ad spend will be handled separately at that stage.

When can I expect to see real results?

While many clients see a spike in engagement and brand presence in month one, our 90-Day Video Funnel is a psychological system built for compounding returns. True market authority and high-ticket inbound leads typically mature by the end of the first 90-day cycle.

Do you work with any real estate agent?

No. We strictly partner with top-tier agents who are ready to scale and willing to invest in their personal brand. This system is not for beginners looking for overnight leads or agents seeking cheap, generic property-tour edits.

How much time do I need to spend recording?

We handle all the heavy lifting strategy, screenplays, setup, and post-production so your time is focused purely on delivery. The required recording time depends on your chosen package and your on-camera presence. Based on standard 8-hour production days, you can expect:

  • Layout Package (12 Videos): 2 Full Production Days / month
  • Landmark Package (15 Videos): 3 Full Production Days / month
  • Legacy Package (24 Videos): 4 to 5 Full Production Days / month

We batch-record these sessions either in our studio or on location to maximize efficiency and maintain peak energy.
